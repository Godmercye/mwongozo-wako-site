KodiTaxes

TIN na kodiTIN and tax

Mfanyabiashara anatakiwa kusajiliwa na Mamlaka ya Mapato Tanzania (TRA) na kupata TIN, kisha kuwasilisha na kulipa kodi inayomhusu.

A business must register with the Tanzania Revenue Authority (TRA), get a TIN, then file and pay the taxes that apply to it.

RisitiReceipts

Muuzaji anatakiwa kutoa risiti ya kielektroniki (EFD) kwa kila mauzo, na mnunuzi anatakiwa kuidai. Hifadhi risiti kama kumbukumbu.

A seller must issue an electronic fiscal receipt (EFD) for each sale, and the buyer should ask for it. Keep receipts as records.

Kupinga tathminiDisputing an assessment

Usipokubali tathmini ya kodi, unaweza kuwasilisha pingamizi kwa TRA, kisha kukata rufaa kwenye Bodi ya Rufaa za Kodi. Zingatia muda uliowekwa.

If you disagree with a tax assessment you can lodge an objection with TRA, then appeal to the Tax Revenue Appeals Board. Watch the deadlines.

Sheria husika: Sheria ya Usimamizi wa Kodi na Sheria ya Kodi ya Mapato.Relevant laws: the Tax Administration Act and the Income Tax Act.
