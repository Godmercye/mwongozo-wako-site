Barabarani na magariRoads and driving

Leseni na bimaLicence and insurance

Dereva anatakiwa kuwa na leseni halali ya udereva, na gari liwe na bima na ukaguzi unaotakiwa. Ubebe nyaraka hizi unapoendesha.

A driver needs a valid driving licence, and the vehicle needs insurance and any required inspection. Carry these documents when driving.

Ukisimamishwa na polisiIf stopped by police

Tii maelekezo kwa utulivu, uliza kosa unalotuhumiwa nalo, na lipa faini kupitia njia rasmi pekee. Omba risiti au namba ya malipo.

Follow instructions calmly, ask what offence you are accused of, and pay any fine only through official channels. Ask for a receipt or control number.

AjaliAccidents

Ukihusika kwenye ajali, toa msaada kwa majeruhi, ripoti polisi na uwasiliane na kampuni yako ya bima. Usiondoke eneo la tukio.

If you are in an accident, help the injured, report to the police and contact your insurer. Do not leave the scene.

Sheria husika: Sheria ya Usalama Barabarani.Relevant law: the Road Traffic Act.
