Mikopo na madeniLoans and debt

Kabla ya kukopaBefore you borrow

Soma mkataba wote na uulize riba, ada zote, muda wa kulipa na adhabu ya kuchelewa. Kopa kutoka kwa taasisi zilizosajiliwa na zenye leseni.

Read the whole agreement and ask about the interest rate, all fees, the repayment period and late penalties. Borrow from registered, licensed lenders.

DhamanaCollateral

Ukiweka mali kama dhamana, mkopeshaji hawezi kuiuza bila kufuata utaratibu wa kisheria na kukupa notisi. Hifadhi nakala za nyaraka zote.

If you pledge property as security, the lender cannot sell it without following the legal process and giving you notice. Keep copies of every document.

Unyanyasaji wa madeniDebt harassment

Mdai ana haki ya kudai deni lake kwa njia za kisheria, lakini si kwa vitisho, kudhalilisha au kuchukua mali yako kinyume cha sheria.

A lender may claim a debt by lawful means, but not by threats, humiliation or taking your property unlawfully.

Sheria husika: Sheria ya Mikataba na sheria za huduma ndogo za fedha.Relevant laws: the Law of Contract Act and microfinance regulations.
