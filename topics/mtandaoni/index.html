Usalama mtandaoniStaying safe online

Makosa ya mtandaoCyber offences

Kusambaza picha au taarifa za mtu bila ridhaa, vitisho, au kuingilia mifumo ya wengine kunaweza kuwa kosa kisheria. Fikiria kabla ya kutuma.

Sharing someone's photos or private information without consent, making threats, or breaking into others' systems can be a crime. Think before you post or forward.

Ulaghai wa simuPhone fraud

Usitume pesa au PIN kwa mtu asiyejulikana. Ukitapeliwa, ripoti haraka kwa mtoa huduma wako na kituo cha polisi, ukiwa na ujumbe na namba za miamala.

Never send money or share your PIN with someone you don't know. If scammed, report quickly to your provider and the police, with the messages and transaction numbers.

Taarifa binafsiPersonal data

Una haki ya kujua taarifa zako zinatumikaje. Malalamiko yanaweza kupelekwa kwa Tume ya Ulinzi wa Taarifa Binafsi (PDPC).

You have a right to know how your data is used. Complaints can go to the Personal Data Protection Commission (PDPC).

Sheria husika: Sheria ya Makosa ya Mtandao na Sheria ya Ulinzi wa Taarifa Binafsi.Relevant laws: the Cybercrimes Act and the Personal Data Protection Act.

Hii ni taarifa ya jumla, si ushauri wa kisheria. Kwa kesi yako, wasiliana na wakili au ofisi ya msaada wa kisheria.This is general information, not legal advice. For your own case, contact a lawyer or legal aid office.
