Vitambulisho na nyarakaIDs and documents

Cheti cha kuzaliwaBirth certificate

Usajili wa kuzaliwa unafanywa na RITA. Cheti ni msingi wa nyaraka nyingine kama shule, kitambulisho na pasipoti, kwa hiyo mtoto asajiliwe mapema.

Births are registered by RITA. The certificate is the basis for other documents such as school records, ID and passport, so register a child early.

Kitambulisho cha TaifaNational ID

NIDA husajili raia na kutoa namba ya kitambulisho. Hifadhi namba yako na usiishiriki kwa watu wasiojulikana.

NIDA registers citizens and issues the ID number. Keep your number safe and do not share it with strangers.

PasipotiPassport

Pasipoti hutolewa na Idara ya Uhamiaji. Tumia tovuti na ofisi rasmi tu, na epuka watu wanaoahidi kukupatia kwa njia za mkato.

Passports are issued by the Immigration Department. Use official websites and offices only, and avoid people who promise shortcuts.

Sheria husika: Sheria ya Usajili wa Kuzaliwa na Vifo na Sheria ya Usajili na Utambuzi wa Watu.Relevant laws: the Births and Deaths Registration Act and the Registration and Identification of Persons Act.
