Ukatili wa kifamiliaDomestic violence

Ukatili ni niniWhat counts as violence

Kupigwa, kutishiwa, kudhalilishwa, kulazimishwa kingono au kunyimwa mahitaji ya msingi ni ukatili. Mengi ya haya ni makosa ya jinai, hata yakitokea ndani ya ndoa au familia.

Beating, threats, humiliation, forced sex or denial of basic needs are forms of violence. Many of these are criminal offences, even inside a marriage or family.

Hatua za kwanzaFirst steps

Tafuta mahali salama na mtu unayemwamini. Ripoti polisi; vituo vingi vina dawati la jinsia na watoto. Hifadhi ushahidi kama picha, ujumbe na ripoti ya matibabu (fomu ya PF3 kutoka polisi).

Get to a safe place and a person you trust. Report to the police; many stations have a gender and children's desk. Keep evidence such as photos, messages and a medical report (the police PF3 form).

Msaada zaidiMore support

Maafisa ustawi wa jamii na mashirika ya msaada wa kisheria wanaweza kukusaidia kupata ulinzi, malezi ya watoto na matibabu.

Social welfare officers and legal aid organisations can help with protection, child care arrangements and medical care.

Sheria husika: Kanuni ya Adhabu na Sheria ya Ndoa.Relevant laws: the Penal Code and the Law of Marriage Act.
