Haki za mgonjwaPatient rights

Matibabu ya dharuraEmergency care

Mtu mwenye hali ya dharura anastahili kupewa huduma ya kwanza bila kucheleweshwa kwa sababu ya fedha au nyaraka.

A person in an emergency should receive first aid without delay over money or paperwork.

UsiriConfidentiality

Taarifa zako za afya ni za siri. Watoa huduma hawatakiwi kuzitoa kwa watu wengine bila ridhaa yako, isipokuwa pale sheria inapoagiza.

Your health information is confidential. Providers should not share it with others without your consent, unless the law requires it.

MalalamikoComplaints

Ukiona huduma si ya haki, anza kwa mkuu wa kituo cha afya, kisha ofisi ya afya ya wilaya, na baraza la kitaaluma husika.

If you think care was improper, start with the head of the facility, then the district health office and the relevant professional council.

Sheria husika: Katiba na sheria za taaluma za afya.Relevant laws: the Constitution and health professions laws.

Hii ni taarifa ya jumla, si ushauri wa kisheria. Kwa kesi yako, wasiliana na wakili au ofisi ya msaada wa kisheria.This is general information, not legal advice. For your own case, contact a lawyer or legal aid office.
