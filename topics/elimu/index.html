Elimu na shuleEducation and school

Haki ya kusomaRight to school

Kila mtoto ana haki ya kupata elimu. Wazazi na walezi wana wajibu wa kuhakikisha watoto wanasoma, na serikali hutoa elimu ya msingi bila ada.

Every child has a right to education. Parents and guardians must make sure children attend, and government provides fee-free basic education.

Nidhamu shuleniSchool discipline

Adhabu shuleni lazima iwe ya wastani na kwa taratibu zilizowekwa. Kupiga kupita kiasi au kudhalilisha mtoto ni ukatili.

Discipline at school must be moderate and follow set procedures. Excessive beating or humiliating a child is abuse.

Mimba na kuendelea na shulePregnancy and returning to school

Sera ya serikali sasa inaruhusu wanafunzi waliopata ujauzito kurudi shule baada ya kujifungua. Uliza ofisi ya elimu ya wilaya kwa maelekezo.

Government policy now allows students who became pregnant to return to school after giving birth. Ask the district education office for guidance.
